Kareem Learning Explorer

Made for children who feel the world loudly.

Most children's apps compete for attention with bright colours, timers and rewards. For many autistic children that is exactly what makes learning impossible. So we built the opposite.

  • One idea per screenA sentence, a picture, and Back, Hear and Next buttons that never move.
  • Read aloud, gentlyA warm teacher's voice, with Arabic phrases spoken properly.
  • Dim the lightControls for brightness, sound, motion, contrast and text size.
  • Nothing to win or loseNo scores, stars, ads or sign-up. A child can't fail a lesson.
