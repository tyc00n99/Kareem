Kareem Learning Explorer

Built for children who feel the world loudly

Most kids' apps are bright, fast and full of rewards. Ours is the opposite on purpose.

  • One idea per screenA sentence, a picture, and fixed Back, Hear and Next buttons that never move.
  • Tap to hearEvery line is read by a gentle teacher's voice, with Arabic phrases spoken properly.
  • Dim the lightSensory settings for sound, movement, contrast, text size and brightness.
  • No scores, ads or sign-upNo stars to chase, no timers and nothing to buy. A child can't fail a lesson.
